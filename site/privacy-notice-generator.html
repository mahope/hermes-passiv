Why you need a privacy notice

  • It is legally required. GDPR Articles 13 and 14 oblige every organisation that processes personal data to tell people about it, at or before collection. Missing or misleading information is one of the most common findings in supervisory-authority inspections.
  • Clients ask for it. Enterprise customers doing vendor assessments routinely request a link to your privacy policy before signing. Having one ready removes friction from sales.
  • It keeps you honest. Writing down what you actually do with data — and checking it once a year — is half of practical GDPR compliance.
