Questions

Who are these guides for?

Small web agencies, freelancers and site owners who need to know what GDPR, NIS2 and the European Accessibility Act require of them — without hiring a consultant first.

Are all guides really free?

Yes. Every guide is free to read, and the companion tools (the scanner CLI and Clean Copy) are free browser-based software.

Which topics are covered?

GDPR (cookies, DPAs, fines), NIS2 (readiness, incident reports, supply chain) and the EAA (deadlines, statements, audits) — plus platform-specific accessibility guides for WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace and more.
