How it works, in short

Install the free Clean Copy extension for Chrome or Firefox, point at what you want, and choose Copy Table or Copy Clean Text. What lands on your clipboard is real structure — an actual table, or plain text — so it pastes correctly wherever it goes next.

Questions

Is all of this free?

Yes. The guides use the free Clean Copy browser extension for Chrome and Firefox. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Do my data leave my computer?

No. The clipboard work happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded until you paste it somewhere yourself.

Which destinations are covered?

Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, LibreOffice Calc, Notion, Airtable, Obsidian and Word — plus plain-text and Markdown workflows.
