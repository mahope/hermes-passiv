How to adapt it — honestly

  1. Replace every [bracketed] field.
  2. Edit the purposes table so it lists what your site actually does. A policy describing processing you don't do is a violation waiting to be found.
  3. Name your real processors (hosting, email, analytics) — each needs an Art. 28 data processing agreement.
  4. Set concrete retention periods, not "as long as necessary".
  5. Publish with a visible link in your footer and a "last updated" date.

When a template is not enough

  • You process special-category data (health, biometrics) — get proper advice.
  • You monitor behaviour at scale or are a public body — a DPIA may be mandatory.
  • You transfer data outside the EU/EEA without an adequacy decision — SCCs need real attention.
