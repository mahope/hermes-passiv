Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the free scanner?

The free scanner gives you a quick grade with basic findings. The Premium Report combines accessibility, GDPR/cookie, and NIS2/security into one document, includes an executive summary, prioritized fix list, and is formatted as a professional PDF you can hand to clients or file for compliance documentation.

Is this a legal compliance guarantee?

No. An automated scan catches roughly 30–40% of accessibility issues and provides a useful baseline, but it cannot replace a full manual audit by a qualified accessibility specialist. This report is a due-diligence tool, not a certification.

Which standards does it check?

EAA / WCAG 2.1 AA (16 automated rules), GDPR cookie consent requirements, and basic NIS2 security posture (CSP, HSTS, HTTPS enforcement). Each finding links to the specific criterion.

Can I use this for client handovers?

Yes — the professional PDF report is designed to be handed to clients, stakeholders, or compliance officers. It includes an executive summary and prioritized action items.

What happens to the pages I scan?

Nothing. The page is fetched server-side through our proxy, analysed in your browser, and immediately discarded. No logs, no storage, no cookies.
