Three regulations, one problem

Since 2025, EU web agencies face three overlapping compliance requirements. Enterprise clients expect proof — and small agencies rarely have the documentation ready.

🔴 NIS2 — Cybersecurity

Enterprise clients must assess vendor security. Without a documented security policy, incident response plan, and contract clauses, your agency looks like a liability.

📋 GDPR — Data Protection

Every agency handling client data needs a Data Processing Agreement (DPA). Clients increasingly demand it as a condition of engagement.

⚡ EAA — Accessibility

Public-facing websites must carry an accessibility statement and meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Agencies serving EU enterprises need to demonstrate compliance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NIS2 and why should a small agency care?

The EU's Network and Information Security Directive 2 requires medium/large enterprises in covered sectors to secure their supply chain. If you serve enterprise clients, they will audit your security practices — and you need documented policies to pass contract requirements.

Does the EAA apply to websites built for clients?

Yes. If a site offers a service or product to the EU public, it must meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards. This includes WooCommerce stores, booking systems, and membership sites. Enforcement is ramping up across EU member states in 2026.
