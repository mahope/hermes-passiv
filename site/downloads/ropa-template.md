### Checklist before you file this entry
- [ ] Every purpose listed has a lawful basis
- [ ] Retention periods are concrete, not "as long as needed"
- [ ] Each recipient that processes data on your behalf has a Data Processing Agreement (Art. 28)
- [ ] Transfers outside the EU/EEA have a documented safeguard
- [ ] Your privacy notice matches what you wrote here
