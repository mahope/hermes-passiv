Clean Copy Pro

Activate your license

Your key is the 32-character string in the order email from orders@mahoje.dk or on the thank-you page after checkout. Use the instructions for the client where you use Clean Copy.

Chrome, Edge or Brave

  1. Open the Clean Copy extension from the browser toolbar.
  2. Open its settings. You can also right-click the extension and choose Options.
  3. Paste your key into License key and select Activate.

Firefox

  1. Open the Clean Copy extension from the Firefox toolbar or Add-ons Manager.
  2. Select Options on the extension card.
  3. Paste your key into License key and select Activate.

Obsidian

  1. Open Settings → Clean Copy in Obsidian.
  2. Paste your key in Clean Copy Pro license.
  3. Select Activate and wait for the Pro status.

Web tool

  1. Open the Clean Copy web tool.
  2. Expand Already have a license key? Activate it here.
  3. Paste your key and select Activate.

Cannot find the key? It is in the receipt email and on the checkout thank-you page. Find a missing license key.

No key yet? You do not need one. The free version is the complete tool — no account, no expiry, no limit — and the only things a Pro key adds are batch conversion and custom cleanup rules in the extension. See free and Pro side by side.
